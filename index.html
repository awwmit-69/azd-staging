AZD Show-Up Guarantee™ — terms

Advertised loudly. Defined clearly.

Guaranteed to show — or you don't pay for that appointment. If a qualifying appointment doesn't attend under the agreed campaign terms, the client isn't charged for that appointment or receives an approved replacement, per the signed service agreement. Key terms:

  • What counts as a qualified appointment (meets agreed filters + a set time).
  • What counts as a show (present & available for the agreed window).
  • Required confirmation process before each appointment.
  • Required appointment window inside your stated hours.
  • Rescheduling and client-cancellation rules.
  • Client availability & sales-capacity requirements.
  • Invalid / unreachable appointment handling.
  • Duplicate-customer de-duplication.
  • Territory & service eligibility.
  • Replacement vs. non-payment (per package).
  • Client-caused exceptions.
  • Documentation & window required to report a no-show.

Full written terms are provided with your service agreement. Draft business terms — subject to counsel review.
